# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Dust Trail a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on what is best for the community

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported by opening a GitHub issue or contacting the maintainer. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
